# Privacy Policy

This Chrome extension accesses and processes limited user data from Slack workspaces solely to provide its functionality.

## Data Collection

The extension accesses basic Slack user profile information via the Slack API. This may include fields such as email address, user ID, username, and account status (e.g., active or deactivated).

If you purchase a license for this extension, certain purchase or licensing information may be collected and processed by a third-party payment provider.

## Purpose of Use

Slack user data is used exclusively to display and analyze workspace user information within the extension.

Purchase and licensing information is used solely to manage access to paid features.

No data is used for advertising, profiling, or tracking purposes.

## Data Storage

Slack user data is processed and stored locally on the user’s device using browser storage. This data is not transmitted, uploaded, or stored on external servers.

License or purchase-related data may be handled by a third-party payment provider and is not stored by the extension itself beyond what is necessary to verify license status.

## Data Sharing

Slack user data is not shared, sold, or transferred to any third parties.

Payment and licensing information is processed by a third-party provider for the purpose of handling transactions and license management.

## Data Retention

Slack user data remains on the user’s device and can be removed at any time by clearing browser storage or uninstalling the extension.

Retention of payment or licensing data is governed by the third-party payment provider.

## Security

Slack user data remains on the user’s device and is not transmitted externally by the extension.

Payment processing is handled by a third-party provider using their own security measures.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
